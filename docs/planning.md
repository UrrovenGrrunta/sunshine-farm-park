# Website planning

## Purpose

The website promotes Sunshine Farm Park as a welcoming visitor attraction and useful stopping point between Edinburgh and Carlisle. It gives potential visitors clear information about the location, opening times and available goods and services. Its main goal is to encourage visits while helping people plan a safe and comfortable day out.

## Requirements list

### Functional requirements

- Provide five consistently designed and mutually linked pages.
- Advertise the farm shop, petting zoo, café, play park and walks.
- Display opening times, sample contact details and location information.
- Include clear navigation on desktop and mobile screens.
- Provide a map link, clickable telephone number and clickable email address.
- Include an expandable FAQ section.
- Allow visitors to compare three sample walking routes.
- Update the copyright year automatically.

### Non-functional requirements

- Use only HTML5, CSS and JavaScript.
- Work in current versions of Chrome, Firefox, Edge and Safari.
- Adapt to desktop, tablet and mobile screen sizes.
- Use readable contrast, visible keyboard focus and semantic page landmarks.
- Support keyboard operation and reduced-motion preferences.
- Load without third-party frameworks or external media dependencies.
- Present a fun but professional countryside identity.

## Target audience

- Families looking for an affordable day out.
- Children interested in animals and outdoor play.
- Garden enthusiasts looking for plants and local produce.
- Tourists and day trippers exploring southern Scotland.
- Travellers seeking a quality place to stop between cities.
- Local residents looking for a café, shop or countryside walk.
- Older visitors and people with accessibility requirements who need clear practical information.

## Navigational structure

```text
Home
├── Visit us
│   ├── Opening times
│   ├── Location
│   ├── Facilities
│   └── FAQs
├── Farm shop
│   ├── Local produce
│   ├── Plants
│   └── Gifts
├── Animals & play
│   ├── Petting zoo
│   ├── Play park
│   └── Safety guidance
└── Café & walks
    ├── Café information
    └── Walking routes
```

Every page contains the complete primary navigation, so visitors can move directly from any page to any other main page.

## Design approach

The supplied outline uses a centred logo, changing image strip, five-link navigation bar, main content area and shared footer. The finished design preserves that structure while introducing a more contemporary visual system. Forest green, leaf green, sunshine yellow, cream and natural brown communicate farming, food and outdoor activity. Rounded cards and friendly illustrations add fun, while restrained typography and generous spacing keep the site professional.

The desktop layout uses two- and three-column content blocks. At smaller screen widths these columns stack, the image strip shows fewer panels and the navigation becomes a clearly labelled menu button.
